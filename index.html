How it works

Four steps, all local. Nothing is sent anywhere at any point.

1

Capture

The full page, scrolled and stitched automatically. Or the visible area, or a region you drag.

2

Detect

Emails, phone numbers, card numbers, tax IDs, keys and tokens — found and rated, highest risk first.

3

Redact

All at once, high-risk only, or item by item. Solid black painted into the image. The pixels are gone.

4

Export

A plain PNG, or a PDF proof report carrying the source URL, page title and capture time.

Privacy

The short version, in plain terms. The full policy has the detail.

  • No network requests. The extension does not contact any server. There is no server to contact.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing about you is gathered, profiled, or sent anywhere.
  • Your capture is deleted as soon as the editor opens it. It is written to local storage purely to hand it from the background worker to the editor tab, then removed immediately.
  • No host permissions. The extension cannot run on pages you have not explicitly asked it to capture.
  • Exports go where you put them. Files are saved to your own device by your browser's normal download flow.
On detection: pattern matching catches common formats, but it is an assistant, not a guarantee. Unusual formats can be missed and ordinary text is occasionally flagged. Review every capture before sharing it — the manual redaction tool is there for anything the scan does not catch.
